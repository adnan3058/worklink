Who we are

A Canadian business with a people-centred outlook.

Worklink Talent is a business name registered in Ontario to 1001726791 Ontario Inc. Our principal place of business is in Mississauga.

We are establishing our presence in the staffing and recruitment market with a simple point of view: employers deserve clarity about hiring needs, and candidates deserve clarity about the opportunities they explore.

Our website is a starting point for those conversations. We invite employers and job seekers to share their requirements so that we can discuss what comes next.

Our perspective

Three principles behind
every conversation.

01 / LISTEN

Understand first

We start with the actual requirements of a role or job search rather than assuming every need is the same.

02 / COMMUNICATE

Keep it clear

Practical information, realistic expectations and straightforward next steps help everyone make informed decisions.

03 / CONNECT

Look for alignment

Skills, working conditions, location and longer-term goals all influence whether a connection makes sense.
